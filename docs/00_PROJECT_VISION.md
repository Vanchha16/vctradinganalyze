# Project Vision

## Overview

ClaudeTrading AI is an AI-powered trading analysis platform that helps traders make informed decisions by combining:

- Live market data
- Technical analysis
- Smart Money Concepts (SMC)
- Economic calendar events
- Financial news sentiment
- Artificial Intelligence reasoning

The platform does NOT execute trades.

Instead, it provides professional Buy / Sell / Wait recommendations with transparent explanations and confidence scores.

---

# Mission

Provide institutional-quality market analysis that is understandable by retail traders.

---

# Vision

Become one of the most trusted AI trading analysis platforms by focusing on transparency, explainability, and data-driven insights rather than "magic" signals.

---

# Core Principles

## Explain Every Decision

Every recommendation must include the reasons behind it.

Example:

BUY EURUSD

Confidence: 86%

Reasons:

- Bullish market structure
- H4 demand zone
- CPI weaker than forecast
- USD sentiment bearish
- Liquidity sweep confirmed
- Strong bullish volume

---

## AI Never Guesses

The AI only reasons over provided data.

It must never invent:

- Prices
- Economic events
- Indicators
- Support/resistance levels

---

## Evidence First

Every recommendation must reference:

Technical indicators

Market structure

Economic news

Sentiment

Risk

---

## Human Friendly

Professional traders should enjoy using it.

Beginners should understand it.
