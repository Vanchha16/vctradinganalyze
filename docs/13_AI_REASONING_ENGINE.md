# AI Reasoning Engine

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Objective

The AI Reasoning Engine transforms structured evidence into a professional market analysis.

The AI never performs technical calculations.

The AI never calculates indicators.

The AI never detects Smart Money Concepts.

The AI only reasons over validated evidence produced by other engines.

---

# 2. Responsibilities

The AI shall:

- Explain market conditions
- Explain why a signal exists
- Detect conflicting evidence
- Explain uncertainty
- Recommend BUY / SELL / WAIT
- Explain confidence
- Explain risk
- Generate professional reports
